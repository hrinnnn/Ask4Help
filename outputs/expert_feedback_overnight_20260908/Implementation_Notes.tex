\paragraph{Implementation details.}
For the reported frozen X-VLA probe we use $H=10$, $\Delta=5$, and $M=2$
free action samples with ten denoising steps each. The comparison uses the
first five generated actions after the usual action-space conversion and
clipping, restricted to the eight active action dimensions. Multiplying
both disagreement and its calibration threshold by a common constant
does not change the feedback. Only anchors with complete ten-action expert
targets supply disagreement feedback. Training retains valid tail anchors
using temporal masks.

Let $c$ and $s$ be the ID feature mean and global RMS deviation, and define
$\widetilde z=(z^B-c)/s$. The local support radius is twice the ID 95th
percentile of nearest-neighbor distances when neighbors from the same
episode are excluded. We use
\begin{equation}
w_{ti}=\mathbf{1}[\|\widetilde z_t-\widetilde z_i\|_2\le\rho]
\exp\!\left(-\frac{\|\widetilde z_t-\widetilde z_i\|_2^2}{2\rho^2}\right),
\end{equation}
with $\lambda=2$ and $\beta=0.5$. At least two source interventions must
support a query, and the weighted mean signed direction must have magnitude
at least $0.25$ before applying the adjustment. These rules were frozen
before the corresponding fresh-seed evaluation.

Translation and gripper reversal use $\sigma_p=0.02$ m and
$\sigma_w=0.01$ m. Their aggregate reference is the ID 95th percentile,
computed over eligible adjacent execution blocks. In the measured setting,
the baseline PCA threshold is $1.70736$, the trajectory-maximum free-action
reference is $11.88798$ under the implementation's $100\times$ scaling, and
the reversal reference is $1.90046$. These values are model-specific and
must be recalibrated when the policy or inference protocol changes.

Let $t_s$ be the first query in an episode at which the baseline requests
assistance but the feedback gate waits. If such a query occurs, the waiting
deadline is $t_s+\Delta$. The final rule is
\begin{equation}
g_t=\mathbf{1}[s_{\mathrm{BPCA}}(o_t)>\gamma_t]
\lor\mathbf{1}[t_s\text{ exists and }t\ge t_s+\Delta],
\end{equation}
evaluated only while the episode is active. After takeover the expert
completes the episode subject to the same cost limit. Censored disagreement
generates a wait cue only when at least five contiguous complete target
windows have low disagreement. A reversal cue requires both complete
motion blocks; at reset it is unavailable.

The local regression formalizes the implemented directional-kernel variant.
Other tested variants include hard threshold-interval projection and
unbounded waiting; their outcomes remain separate. Adaptive thresholds
need not retain the baseline false-alarm calibration. Our experiments
record total executed expert actions, including unsuccessful interventions,
separately from retained actions for subsequent training. Additional frozen
inference for feedback is also a computation cost.
