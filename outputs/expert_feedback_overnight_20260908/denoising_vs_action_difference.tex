\paragraph{Denoising error and execution disagreement.}
These signals answer different questions. In an expert-conditioned
reconstruction objective, a noisy target
$x_s=(1-s)a^*+s\epsilon$ is supplied to the action model, and the model
minimizes an error such as
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rec}}
=\mathbb E_{s,\epsilon}
  \|D_\theta(o,x_s,s)-a^*\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
The noisy input contains information about the expert target, and the
difficulty of reconstruction depends on the noise level. Consequently,
small reconstruction error need not imply that the freely executed policy
would select the expert action. The precise objective and output
parameterization are backbone-specific; the expression above describes
clean-action reconstruction rather than every flow or diffusion objective.

For timing feedback we instead compare actions generated by the normal
inference procedure with the recorded expert actions. For a stochastic
policy this discrepancy decomposes as
\begin{equation}
\mathbb E\|\widehat a-a^*\|_2^2
=\|\mathbb E\widehat a-a^*\|_2^2
 +\mathbb E\|\widehat a-\mathbb E\widehat a\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
It therefore captures systematic disagreement even when action samples
have low variance. Comparing the first $\Delta$ actions of a generated
chunk makes the feedback correspond to the block actually executed before
the next policy query. A different valid expert action may still yield
large disagreement, so this signal provides evidence about the current
policy's agreement with the observed supervision rather than a direct
estimate of failure probability or training gain.

\paragraph{An operational preferred region.}
For a hypothetical intervention at $t$, let $A(t)$ denote the length of
the initial expert prefix before the first elevated action disagreement,
and let $R(t)$ denote the measured reversal of the preceding robot motion.
An operational preferred region is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{FB}}(\tau)
=\{t:A(t)<\Delta,\ R(t)\le q_R\}.
\end{equation}
This region expresses a design preference: expert-specific behavior should
be needed within the next execution block, while substantial initial
correction should be avoided. It need not be a single interval or be
nonempty. The algorithm observes these quantities only at actual
interventions and transfers their local feedback through representation
similarity; it does not enumerate counterfactual expert continuations.
Missing boundaries are censored observations, and evidence for one
low-disagreement block cannot justify unbounded postponement. Membership
in this operational region remains a proxy, distinct from the takeover
time that maximizes post-update success under a fixed expert budget.
